Privacy Policy

The protection of your personal health information is essential to us. Here is how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

The protection of your personal information is very important to us. All Registered Physiotherapists at Core Connections Physiotherapy are required to comply with the Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA), which safeguards the confidentiality of personal and health information.

Your personal information is collected and shared only with your explicit written consent, except where disclosure is required by law.

Electronic Software & Practice Management

Clinical records are stored securely and electronically using Jane App, a PHIPA-compliant practice management system. Access to your records is limited strictly to authorized individuals who require access to perform their roles and who adhere to professional privacy standards (administrative staff, technical support when required, College audits, or legal mandates).

Secure Communication & Storage

  • Fax Service: We utilize a secure, encrypted fax service (SRFax) that fully meets PHIPA requirements.
  • Email Protection: Your email address and phone number are never sold or shared with third parties. They are used exclusively for appointment confirmations, reminders, and administrative clinic communication.
  • On-Site Security: Strong passwords protect all clinic computers and medical software. Clinic premises are protected with an alarm system.
  • Paper Files: All physical documents containing personal information are securely shredded.

Transfer of Information

Core Connections Physiotherapy reserves the right to transfer collected data only in the event of a full or partial acquisition of the clinic.
