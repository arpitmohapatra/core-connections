Principles

Decisions are tested against these, in order.

handshakeConsent is never buriedInternal exams, pace and choices are explained before booking, not in fine print.
visibilitySay it plainlyCondition, what we do, next step. "Waitlist: about 3 weeks" beats a vague "contact us".
touch_appThumb first48 px targets. Book and Call sit in the lower third on phones, on every page.
spaCalm, not clinicalSoft tinted surfaces and real clinic photos. No stock anatomy, no alarm colours for routine content.

Colour

Violet ink on lavender-tinted neutrals. Citron marks the one thing to do next, almost always booking. Red is reserved for errors and closures. Every text pair below meets WCAG AA.

Space, shape, elevation

A 4 / 8 grid. Corners: 14 on buttons and inputs, 16 on cards, 20 on photos, 28 on hero panels, full pill on chips and badges. Shadows are tinted violet, never pure black.

Do and don't

The mistakes that most often make a health clinic site feel cold or confusing.

check_circleDo
Keep Book online visible in the header and the mobile action bar on every page.Use real clinic and team photos. Put white text on the darker, lower part of the image.Use "Read more" rows to shorten long pages.
cancelDon't
Mix the two palettes on one page. Cross-links use the other clinic's soft badge only.Use red for anything except errors and closures.Signal status with colour alone. Always add an icon and a word.
