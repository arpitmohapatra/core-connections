Welcome

Welcome to Core Connections Physiotherapy!

We have been providing integrated pelvic health and orthopedic physiotherapy since 2008. As a community-based, physiotherapist-owned clinic in the heart of Westboro, we’re proud to provide compassionate, hands-on care grounded in clinical excellence.
