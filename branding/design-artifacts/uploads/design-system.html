Design system v1.0 for Flutter

Built for the ride home.

Dhruva is the commute product for companies that move people at night. Calm teal, clear type, and a safety kit that is always one thumb away. Three surfaces share one set of tokens: Rider, Driver, and Control Room.

Principles

Decisions are tested against these, in order.

sos
Safety is never buried
SOS, share and OTP are visible on every live-trip screen, never behind a menu.
visibility
Say it plainly
State, reason, next step. "Closed for today" beats a grey button.
touch_app
Thumb first
48 dp targets, primary action in the lower third, no gesture-only paths.
density_small
Dense where it pays
Control Room packs 13 px data; mobile breathes at 15 px.

Colour

Teal ink on cool neutrals. Amber marks the one thing to do next. Red is reserved for SOS and expired items, escort is a distinct magenta so it never reads as danger. Every text pair meets WCAG AA in both themes.

Space, shape, elevation

A 4 / 8 grid. Corners: 14 on buttons and inputs, 16 on cards, 24 on sheets, full pill on chips and badges. Shadows are tinted teal, never pure black in light mode.

Motion

Motion explains cause and effect: a sheet rises from its trigger, a screen slides in the direction of travel, SOS counts down visibly. Nothing loops except live pulses. Reduced motion collapses every duration to zero.

Iconography

Material Symbols Rounded, weight 400, optical size 24. Outline by default, filled (FILL 1) for the active tab and for safety icons. Sizes: 16 inline, 20 in buttons, 24 default, 32 empty states. Never mix another family.

Do and don't

The mistakes that most often make a safety product look careless.

check_circleDo
Keep SOS visible on every live-trip and trip-run screen.
event_busyExpiredPair colour with an icon and a word.
cancelDon't
more_horizHide SOS in an overflow menu or behind a long press only.
Signal status with a lone coloured dot.
check_circleDo
Explain blocked actions: "Cannot assign: fitness certificate expired 12 Oct."Ask for a reason on every manual roster change.
cancelDon't
Use red for anything but SOS, expired, errors and destructive actions.Use amber text on white; use the warn token (contrast 5:1).
