Careers & mentorship

Join our team!

Looking for a clinic where you can grow, collaborate, and truly love what you do?

Pelvic Health & Orthopedics Physiotherapist

Part-time · 15–30 hours/week · Permanent position

At Core Connections Physiotherapy, you’ll work alongside an experienced, supportive team providing thoughtful, hands-on, integrated pelvic health and orthopedic care.

What makes this opportunity exciting?

  • A varied caseload including pelvic health, perinatal, orthopedic manual therapy, and vestibular physiotherapy
  • Opportunities to deepen your clinical reasoning and develop an integrated, whole-person approach
  • Team in-services, ongoing mentorship, and collaborative learning

We’d especially love to hear from you if you have

  • Pelvic health training and ideally 2+ years of experience — or an orthopedic background and enthusiasm for developing your pelvic health skills
  • Strong communication skills and a passion for patient-centred care
  • 3–5+ years of orthopedic manual therapy experience
  • Advanced vestibular training
  • English–French bilingualism

Interested? We’d love to meet you! Send your resume and cover letter to reception@core-connections.ca.

Mentorship

Pelvic Health Clinical Mentorship

We offer clinical mentorship in pelvic health physiotherapy within a supportive, collaborative learning environment. Mentorship can be tailored to your experience and learning goals, with opportunities to strengthen clinical reasoning, assessment and treatment skills, and confidence in managing a range of pelvic health presentations.

Our goal is to support your professional growth through shared clinical knowledge, discussion and practical guidance. Contact us to learn more about mentorship opportunities and how we can support your learning goals.
