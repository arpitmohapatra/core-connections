Cancellation Policy & Fees

To ensure timely care for all patients, we ask for 48 business hours’ notice for cancellations or rescheduling.

48 Business-Hour Cancellation Notice

To cancel or reschedule an appointment, please provide at least 48 business hours’ notice. This notice period allows us to offer the appointment time to another patient awaiting care.

Cancellations must be made by calling reception at (613) 291-2956 (Monday–Friday, 8:30 am to 5:00 pm). We are closed on weekends and do not accept cancellations by email.

How Business Hours Apply

  • Monday appointments: Cancel by 12:00 pm (noon) on Thursday.
  • Tuesday appointments: Cancel by 12:00 pm (noon) on Friday.

Cancellation & Missed Appointment Fees

Our administrative assistant will follow up to arrange payment for late cancellations or missed visits. Payments are accepted via e-transfer to admin@core-connections.ca.

  • Late Cancellations (less than 48 business hours' notice): Subject to a fee equal to 50% of the scheduled appointment cost.
  • Missed Appointments (no-shows): Subject to a fee equal to 100% of the full appointment cost.

Exceptions & Telehealth Alternatives

Policy Exceptions

We understand that emergencies happen. Exceptions may be made for sudden illness, family emergencies, or severe weather conditions. Note: Exceptions cannot be made for work-related conflicts.

Telehealth Option

To avoid a late cancellation fee due to work, childcare, transport issues, or weather, you may switch your in-clinic session to a Telehealth session (video or phone) by calling (613) 291-2956 before your appointment.

Late Arrival Policy

If you are running late, please call to inform us. While we cannot extend scheduled session times, we will provide care during your remaining appointment time. The full appointment fee applies.
