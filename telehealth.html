Telehealth

Telehealth or in-clinic appointments.

A convenient, flexible way to begin or continue working toward your physiotherapy goals from the comfort of home.

It can be particularly helpful for busy parents, people with mobility or transportation challenges, during inclement weather, or when you would like timely guidance. During a telehealth appointment, your physiotherapist can take your history, observe your posture, breathing and movement, provide education and exercise instruction, and monitor your progress. Depending on your needs, an in-clinic appointment may be more appropriate when hands-on assessment or treatment is important.

The best option depends on your symptoms, goals and individual circumstances. Your physiotherapist can help determine the most appropriate approach.

Telehealth may be a good option if you

  • Would benefit from education, exercise instruction or a review of your home program.
  • Prefer to receive care from home or have difficulty travelling to the clinic.
  • Would like to discuss your symptoms and goals with a physiotherapist.
  • Have health-related concerns that make an in-clinic visit less suitable.

In-clinic may be more appropriate if you

  • Would benefit from hands-on assessment or treatment.
  • Are having difficulty managing your symptoms with your current home program.
  • Require an in-person assessment to guide your treatment plan.
Increased risk from infection? If you are immunocompromised or have concerns about attending the clinic because of your health, please contact our reception team at reception@core-connections.ca or call 613-291-2956 to discuss your options.

Not sure which appointment to choose?

We’re happy to help. Contact our reception team to arrange a complimentary phone consultation with a physiotherapist to discuss your needs and the options available.

What is telehealth physiotherapy?

Telehealth physiotherapy, also called virtual care, involves delivering physiotherapy services remotely using secure videoconferencing or, when appropriate, a phone consultation. Depending on your needs, it may include a patient interview, movement assessment, education, exercise prescription and follow-up. Physiotherapists providing telehealth follow the applicable professional standards and practice requirements. Your physiotherapist will determine whether virtual care is appropriate, whether an in-person assessment is needed, and whether your treatment goals can be addressed effectively through telehealth.

Will my insurance cover telehealth physiotherapy?

Coverage varies by insurance provider and plan. We recommend contacting your insurer to confirm whether telehealth physiotherapy is covered and whether a physician’s referral is required.

What do I need for a telehealth appointment?

You will generally need a phone, tablet or computer with a camera, microphone and internet connection. If video is not accessible to you, ask us whether a phone consultation is suitable for your needs.

How secure and private is telehealth?

We use a telehealth platform with encryption to help protect your privacy. Please review our Consent to Telehealth Appointments for further information (ask reception for a copy).
