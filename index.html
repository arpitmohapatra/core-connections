A regional first

Ottawa's first pessary-fitting physiotherapy clinic

We've collaborated with physicians, specialists, and nurse practitioners to fit pessaries since 2013. We carry seven pessary types and take as much trial and error as it takes to find the right fit.

  • Pre-fitting assessment through to long-term follow-up
  • Care offered in English, French, and Telugu
  • Combined with pelvic floor re-education and manual therapy
