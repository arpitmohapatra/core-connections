Our philosophy

We treat the source, not just the symptom.

“We strive to understand and integrate the relationships between the musculoskeletal, myofascial, pelvic health, visceral, biopsychosocial and nervous systems.”

We're proud to be inclusive and sensitive to the diverse needs of all ethnicities, gender identities, gender expression, and sexual orientations. We provide gender-affirming and trauma-informed care, and we've been fitting pessaries for over 13 years as part of that integrated approach.
