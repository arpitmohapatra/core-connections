Welcome to Core Connections

Care that starts with listening.

We are a community-based clinic for pelvic health, pre and postnatal, orthopedic, and sports physiotherapy. Our team combines decades of hands-on experience with an inclusive, collaborative approach to help you understand your body and return to what you love.

How we can help

Expert care for the moments that shape how you move.

Start with the service that feels closest to your needs. If you are unsure, our reception team is happy to help.

01

Pelvic Health Physiotherapy

Expert, respectful care for all genders — women, men, and 2SLGBTQIA+ people.

What we support +

Pelvic pain, bladder and bowel concerns, prolapse, incontinence, sexual health, and post-surgical recovery.

02

Pre & Postnatal Physiotherapy

Integrated orthopedic and pelvic health care for pregnancy, recovery, and a safe return to movement.

What we support +

Pregnancy-related pain, labor prep, postpartum pelvic check, diastasis recti, and return to sport.

03

Orthopedic Manual Therapy

Hands-on assessment and treatment for joints, muscles, and nerves by FCAMPT-certified physiotherapists.

What we support +

Musculoskeletal injuries, back & neck pain, joint stiffness, movement dysfunction, and post-op rehab.

04

Pessary Fitting Services

Ottawa's first physiotherapist-led pessary clinic for conservative prolapse and incontinence care.

What we support +

Pelvic organ prolapse, stress urinary incontinence, pessary selection, fitting, and ongoing maintenance.

05

Vestibular Physiotherapy

Targeted rehabilitation to resolve dizziness, vertigo, unsteadiness, and balance concerns.

What we support +

BPPV, vertigo, dizziness, vestibular neuritis, labyrinthitis, and balance disorders.

06

Physiotherapy for Adults 60+

Specialized care focused on staying strong, active, and independent as your body changes.

What we support +

Osteoarthritis, osteoporosis, balance & fall prevention, mobility enhancement, and joint rehab.

07

Hypermobility & Ehlers-Danlos

Gentle, individualized care designed for Hypermobility Spectrum Disorders (HSD) and EDS.

What we support +

Joint hypermobility, recurrent subluxations, chronic pain, proprioception, and fatigue management.

08

Complimentary Modalities

Focused adjunct treatments that complement and enhance your overall physiotherapy care plan.

What we support +

Pelvic floor biofeedback, cupping therapy, hypopressives, anorectal balloon therapy, and ultrasound.

09

Telehealth Services

Convenient virtual care bringing expert guidance, posture reviews, and exercise progression to your home.

What we support +

Virtual consultations, ergonomic reviews, guided exercise, education, and remote follow-ups.

Our approach

Your goals stay at the centre.

We look beyond symptoms to understand the factors contributing to them. Through hands-on treatment, exercise, education, and thoughtful clinical reasoning, we make room for your experiences and give you tools to move forward.

  • Integrated, individualized treatment
  • Private treatment rooms and full appointment time with your physiotherapist
  • Care available in English, French, Spanish, and Telugu
