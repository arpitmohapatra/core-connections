Welcome

Welcome to Core Connections Physiotherapy!

We have been providing integrated pelvic health and orthopedic physiotherapy since 2008. As a community-based, physiotherapist-owned clinic in the heart of Westboro, we’re proud to provide compassionate, hands-on care grounded in clinical excellence, with a commitment to inclusive, gender-affirming and trauma-informed practice.

We offer our services in English, French, Spanish and Telugu.

Understanding the mind-body connection

We understand how working through fear, trauma, anxiety and emotional overwhelm can impact our bodies.

Building body awareness

We integrate principles of somatic education into our practice, helping you develop greater awareness of your body, movement, sensations, breathing, tension and patterns of response.

Hands-on physiotherapy

We use skilled touch and movement assessment to listen to and understand what your body is telling us — feeling for areas of restriction, changes in tissue mobility, altered movement patterns, and how different parts of the body move and work together.

Collaborative, passionate care

We are a close-knit team of physiotherapists who share knowledge, collaborate on complex cases, and learn from one another. Our different areas of experience and clinical perspectives allow us to draw on the strengths of the team when considering your care.

Our philosophy

We treat the source, not just the symptom.

“We strive to understand and integrate the relationships between the musculoskeletal, myofascial, pelvic health, visceral, biopsychosocial and nervous systems.”

Everyone is welcome. We are inclusive and sensitive to the unique needs of people of any ethnicity, gender identity, gender expression and sexual orientation. Our clinic space is calm and beautiful and the treatment rooms are private.
