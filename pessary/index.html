About pessaries

A familiar service, now with its own name.

Pessaries can provide support for pelvic organ prolapse and may help manage certain types of urinary incontinence. Our pessary fitting services now have a new name: the Ottawa Pessary Clinic.

We have been collaborating with specialists, physicians, other healthcare providers and physio clinics in Ottawa and surrounding areas to fit pessaries since 2013.

How a visit may unfold

A calm, step-by-step path.

Every person’s care is different. This is a general outline of what a pessary fitting journey can include, not a fixed protocol.

  1. Reach out

    Call or email with questions. Complimentary phone consultations are available to help you decide on a next step.

  2. Talk it through

    Share your symptoms, goals and concerns. Questions are part of the visit, and you set the pace.

  3. Assessment, on your terms

    Your assessment may include an internal exam. You always choose whether to have one, to defer it, or not to have one at all.

  4. Fitting, when appropriate

    If a pessary is appropriate, a fitting may follow, alongside pelvic health physiotherapy where it makes sense for you.

  5. Follow-up and teamwork

    Follow-up and collaboration with your healthcare provider are part of pessary care.
