Services

Our goal: help you feel better, move better.

And to help you continue to do the things you love.

Primary services

Where most of our patients start.

Pelvic Health Physiotherapy

Expert and experienced care for pelvic health conditions and wellness — for women, men, and 2SLGBTQIA+ individuals.

Pre & Postnatal Physiotherapy

Integrated orthopedic, pelvic health and pessary fitting services to help with pain, mobility, recovery, and a safe return to sport, before and after childbirth.

Orthopedic & Sports Physiotherapy

Hands-on manual therapy and personalized exercises to reduce pain, restore movement, and prevent recurrence. We are FCAMPT-certified!

Specialty services

Focused expertise for specific conditions.

Specialty

Pessary Fitting Services

Comprehensive pessary care for pelvic organ prolapse and incontinence — including fitting, follow-up, and collaboration with your healthcare provider. We've been fitting pessaries for over 13 years.

Ehlers-Danlos Syndromes & Hypermobility

Treatment focused on joint protection, pain reduction, improving stability, and building strength to support safe, confident movement in daily life.

Vestibular Rehabilitation

Manual therapy and exercise for dizziness and balance, to help you feel confident in your daily activities.
