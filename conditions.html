Pelvic Health Physiotherapy

Individualized care for a wide range of concerns, tailored to your symptoms, needs and goals.

Bladder health

  • Urinary incontinence, urgency and frequency
  • Difficulty emptying the bladder

Pelvic organ prolapse

  • Pelvic heaviness, pressure or bulging
  • Prolapse-related symptoms and support concerns

Bowel & anorectal health

  • Bowel incontinence and urgency
  • Difficulty emptying the bowels
  • Anorectal and pelvic floor concerns

Pelvic pain & muscle tension

  • Endometriosis-related pelvic pain
  • Painful intercourse and other forms of sexual pain
  • Tailbone, pelvic, hip and persistent lower back pain
  • Pelvic floor muscle tension

Sexual health & function

  • Sexual pain or difficulties affecting sexual function
  • Pelvic health concerns affecting comfort and intimacy

Menopause & genitourinary health

  • Genitourinary syndrome of menopause (GSM)
  • Vaginal dryness, irritation and discomfort
  • Urinary and sexual symptoms associated with menopause

Gender-affirming care

  • Pelvic health concerns related to gender-affirming practices
  • Rehabilitation following gender-affirming surgery, including vaginoplasty

Pre- & post-surgical rehabilitation

  • Preparation for and recovery following pelvic or abdominal surgery
  • Scar mobility and related musculoskeletal concerns

Pessary fitting

Pessaries can provide support for pelvic organ prolapse and may help manage certain types of urinary incontinence. We offer individualized pessary fitting and pelvic health physiotherapy through the Ottawa Pessary Clinic.

Pre- & Postnatal Physiotherapy

Pregnancy and childbirth can affect the pelvic floor, abdominal muscles and musculoskeletal system. Our individualized approach supports comfort, movement and recovery.

Pregnancy-related pain

  • Pelvic girdle, back and hip pain
  • Neck and shoulder pain

Abdominal muscle changes

  • Abdominal muscle separation (diastasis recti)
  • Abdominal strength and functional concerns

Pelvic floor health

  • Pelvic organ prolapse symptoms
  • Urinary incontinence
  • Pelvic floor muscle tension, weakness or coordination difficulties

Postnatal recovery

  • Scar tissue following perineal tears or episiotomy
  • Recovery following vaginal birth or caesarean birth
  • Return to exercise and everyday activities

Orthopedic Physiotherapy

Individualized care for musculoskeletal pain, mobility limitations and movement difficulties, helping you return to the activities that matter to you.

Head, neck & shoulder

  • Headaches
  • Neck and shoulder pain

Back & pelvic girdle

  • Mid-back (thoracic) pain
  • Low back and sacroiliac joint pain

Nerve-related symptoms

  • Sciatica and other nerve-related symptoms

Hip, knee, foot & ankle

  • Hip and knee osteoarthritis
  • Foot and ankle pain

Posture & movement

  • Postural and movement-related concerns
  • Repetitive strain injuries

Sports & physical activity

  • Sports injuries
  • Rehabilitation to support return to physical activity

Persistent pain

  • Fibromyalgia and persistent widespread pain

Bone health

  • Osteoporosis and osteopenia

Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome (EDS) & Hypermobility Spectrum Disorders (HSD)

EDS and HSD can affect joint stability, movement, pain and everyday function. Physiotherapy is tailored to your symptoms, abilities and goals.

What we help with

  • Joint hypermobility and instability
  • Joint and muscle pain
  • Recurrent sprains, strains or joint dislocations
  • Fatigue and reduced exercise tolerance
  • Difficulties with joint control, balance or everyday activities
Our approach may include individualized strengthening, joint stability and movement retraining, activity modification, pacing and strategies to support participation in daily life.

Vestibular Physiotherapy

Vestibular physiotherapy can help assess and manage dizziness, vertigo and balance difficulties. Treatment is tailored to your symptoms, their potential contributing factors and your functional goals.

Vestibular & inner-ear conditions

  • Benign paroxysmal positional vertigo (BPPV)
  • Vestibular neuritis and labyrinthitis
  • Ménière’s disease and other inner-ear conditions
  • Vestibular migraine

Dizziness & balance difficulties

  • Dizziness or vertigo triggered by head movement or changes in position
  • Unsteadiness and balance difficulties
  • Age-related changes in balance
  • Motion sensitivity or difficulty in visually busy environments

Concussion & neck-related symptoms

  • Concussion and other head injuries
  • Whiplash and neck-related dizziness
  • Dizziness or balance symptoms following injury

Physiotherapy for Adults 60+

Arthritis, pain & stiffness

  • Osteoarthritis and other musculoskeletal concerns

Bone health

Orthopedic conditions & injuries

  • Nerve-related symptoms
  • Persistent musculoskeletal pain

Balance & fall prevention

  • Balance difficulties and unsteadiness
  • Reduced confidence with walking or everyday activities
  • Support to improve stability, mobility and safety

Dizziness & vestibular conditions

  • Dizziness and vertigo
  • Balance difficulties related to vestibular conditions

Pelvic health

  • Urinary incontinence
  • Pelvic organ prolapse
  • Other pelvic health concerns, including pessary fitting when appropriate (Ottawa Pessary Clinic)
