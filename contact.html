Getting here

Accessibility & parking

Stairs

Our clinic is on the second floor. Three steps at street level (handrails both sides), then one flight of stairs up. Let us know in advance if you have mobility concerns, and we can offer a telehealth appointment or discuss in-home care.

Paid street parking

Richmond Rd in front of the clinic: $3/hour, 2-hour maximum, 7am-7pm. Pay by card, coin, or the PayByPhone app. MEC across the street: $1.50/hour, 2-hour maximum.

Free & accessible parking

Free after 4:30pm on weekdays and anytime on weekends in the Frontline Credit Union lot off Madison Ave. Patients with an accessible sticker or a mobility restriction can use it any time, just let reception know.

By bus

OC Transpo route 11 stops right outside the clinic. Westboro Station is a 5-10 minute walk away, served by all Transitway routes.
