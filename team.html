Meet the team

Heather Grewar

Founder & Physiotherapist

BScPT, MScPT, FCAMPT. Over 29 years as an orthopedic and pelvic health physiotherapist. Founder of Core Connections, with 10 years of postgraduate training in orthopedic manual therapy and a Master's from Queen's exploring the interconnectedness of the musculoskeletal system and pelvic health.

Specialties
Orthopedic manual therapy, pelvic health, pre & postnatal physiotherapy, pessary fitting, Barral Visceral Mobilization
