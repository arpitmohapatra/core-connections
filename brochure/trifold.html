Our approach

Welcoming, inclusive and community-based.

Our focus is to understand the root cause of your symptoms, and treat the source of the problem.

Our goal is to empower you through hands-on treatment, exercise and education.

Mind-body connectionWe understand how fear, trauma and anxiety can affect the body.
Body awarenessSomatic education builds awareness of movement, breath and tension.
Collaborative careOur different areas of expertise inform every treatment plan.
