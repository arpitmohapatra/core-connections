About us

A centre for healing, not just a clinic

Core Connections Physiotherapy is a community-based clinic providing leadership in pelvic health, pre & postnatal care, and orthopedics and sports.

Our story

Eighteen years of growing, one patient at a time

Heather Grewar began developing an integrated pelvic health and orthopedic program in Ottawa in 2008, driven by a strong sense that pelvic health deserved far more attention than it was getting — from physicians, physiotherapy students, and the community alike.

In 2011, that work became Core Connections Physiotherapy: a single sunny room in Wellington West, with Heather as clinician, receptionist, and cleaner all at once. Monate soon joined her, and later Sue, sparking the collaborative team the clinic is built on today.

In 2014, the clinic moved to its current home in Westboro on Richmond Rd, across from MEC — a space with high ceilings, south-west light, and hardwood floors that's hosted countless hours of healing since.

Our philosophy

Treat the cause, not just the symptom

Our focus is understanding the root cause of your symptoms and the factors contributing to them, so we can treat the source of the problem and help prevent it from returning — not just manage what hurts today.

  • Hands-on: your full appointment is with your physiotherapist, start to finish
  • Integrated: we look at the articular, myofascial, neurological, visceral, and pelvic floor systems together
  • Inclusive: welcoming and sensitive to every ethnicity, gender identity, gender expression, and sexual orientation
