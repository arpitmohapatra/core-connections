Accessibility & Parking

Clear guidance on visiting our Ottawa clinic, entering the building, parking options, and accommodating your accessibility needs.

Building Entry & Accessibility

Our clinic is located on the second floor at 363 Richmond Road.

  • Street Entrance: 3 steps up at street level with handrails on both sides into the building entrance.
  • Stairs to 2nd Floor: A main flight of stairs with a railing on the left-hand side leads up to our clinic, with an additional 4 steps inside.

Stair Assistance & Accommodations: If you have any mobility concerns regarding the stairs, please contact us prior to your appointment. We can arrange assistance, offer telehealth appointments, or discuss home visit options.

Free Accessible Parking

Dedicated accessible spots are located in the Frontline Credit Union parking lot directly behind the clinic.

  • Patients with Accessible Stickers: You may park in the rear lot for the duration of your treatment.
  • Patients with Mobility Restrictions: Accessible sticker not required for patients experiencing mobility limitations (pregnancy, seniors, acute injury, or fall risk). Please notify reception or your therapist upon arrival.

Directions to Rear Lot: From Richmond Rd, drive north on Churchill Ave, turn west onto Madison Ave, continue past the church to the south lot with the Frontline Credit Union sign.

Sensory & Communication Accommodations

Hearing Impairments

If you prefer communicating by email, please message our reception with the subject line "Request to Book Through Email Only". We will update your profile and communicate exclusively via email.

Email: reception@core-connections.ca

Visual Impairments

Please call reception to notify us of any visual impairments. We will note your chart and ensure all booking updates and communications are provided verbally.

Phone: (613) 291-2956

Parking Options Summary

Richmond Road Metered Parking

$3.00/hour (maximum 2 hours, 7:00 am – 7:00 pm). Payable by coin, credit card, or the PayByPhone mobile app.

MEC Parking Lot (366 Richmond Rd across street)

$1.50/hour (maximum 2 hours). Credit card, cash, or the IndigoNeo app.

Westboro Station Underground (587 Byron Ave)

$1.50 per 30 minutes ($10.00 daily max). 5-minute walk from the clinic.

Parkopedia Athlone Lot (277 Richmond Rd)

Pay & Display machine or PayByPhone app (call 613-691-1700). 5-minute walk.

Free Evening & Weekend Parking

Free parking in the lot behind the clinic (via Madison Ave) after 4:30 pm on weekdays and anytime on weekends.

Free 3-Hour Street Parking

Free 3-hour parking is available on nearby side streets including Lower Byron, Ravenhill, and Whitby Ave.
