Finding our clinic.

Core Connections Physiotherapy is at 363 Richmond Road in Westboro, Ottawa.

Finding our clinic

We are located at 363 Richmond Road in Westboro, Ottawa, just west of Churchill Avenue on the north side of Richmond Road, directly across from Mountain Equipment Company (MEC). Our clinic has a separate entrance off Richmond Road, beside Frontline Financial Credit Union.

View our location on Google Maps

Public transit

  • OC Transpo Route 11: stops directly outside the clinic.
  • Westboro Station: approximately a 5–10-minute walk from the clinic; served by Transitway routes. For current routes and schedules, visit OC Transpo.

Driving directions

From Highway 417 westbound

Take the Carling Avenue exit. Continue west on Carling Avenue, then turn right onto Churchill Avenue. Follow Churchill Avenue to Richmond Road and turn left. The clinic is on your right, directly across from MEC.

From Highway 417 eastbound

Take the Maitland Avenue exit and head north to Carling Avenue. Turn right onto Carling Avenue, then left onto Churchill Avenue. Follow Churchill Avenue to Richmond Road and turn left. The clinic is on your right, directly across from MEC.

From downtown Ottawa

Common routes include Highway 417 westbound (exiting at Carling Avenue), Scott Street to Churchill Avenue, or the Sir John A. Macdonald Parkway via Island Park Drive.

We look forward to welcoming you to Core Connections Physiotherapy!

Parking

Several parking options are available near the clinic. Rates, time limits and availability may change — please check posted signs and payment instructions when parking.

Free parking

  • Richmond Road: free parking for up to 90 minutes in front of the clinic.
  • Rear parking lot: available on weekdays after 4:30 p.m. and anytime on weekends. Access via Madison Avenue through the Frontline Financial Credit Union lot.
  • Nearby side streets: free parking for up to 3 hours on surrounding streets, including Whitby Avenue, Wilmont Avenue, Lower Byron and Ravenhill. Please check posted signs.

Paid parking

  • MEC, 366 Richmond Road (across the street): listed rate $1.50 per hour, maximum stay 2 hours. Visa, Mastercard, cash or the Indigo Neo app.
  • Westboro Station, 587 Byron Avenue (underground, about a 5-minute walk): listed rate $1.50 per 30 minutes, up to $10 per day. PayByPhone or card at pay stations.
  • Athlone lot, 277 Richmond Road (about a 5-minute walk): check posted rates and payment instructions.
  • Richmond Road in front of the clinic: paid street parking is also available; check the posted signs or payment machine.

Please confirm current rates and restrictions on-site.

Accessibility

Getting into the clinic

Stairs

Our clinic is located on the second floor and is accessible by stairs only.

  • Street-level entrance: three steps with handrails on both sides.
  • Staircase to the clinic: one flight of stairs with a handrail on the left when ascending, followed by an additional four steps.

If you have difficulty with stairs or are unsure whether you can access the clinic comfortably, please contact us before your appointment. We can discuss options, including telehealth or physiotherapy services in your home.

Accessible parking & assistance with mobility

We have arranged parking options behind the clinic for patients who need assistance with access. Patients with an accessible parking permit can use the designated accessible parking spaces at the south end of the rear parking lot for the duration of their appointment. Patients with temporary or ongoing mobility restrictions — including those related to pregnancy, injury, balance difficulties or age-related mobility changes — are welcome to contact us to discuss parking arrangements. Please let our receptionist or your physiotherapist know in advance so we can coordinate with the landlord.

Directions to the rear parking lot

  1. From Richmond Road, head north on Churchill Avenue.
  2. Turn west onto Madison Avenue.
  3. Continue past the church to the parking lot on the south side, marked with a Frontline Financial Credit Union sign.
  4. The designated accessible parking spaces are at the south end of the lot.

Communication accommodations

We want to make communication and appointment booking as accessible as possible. Please let us know if you have a hearing or visual impairment that affects how you prefer to communicate, and we will do our best to accommodate your needs.

Hearing impairments (unable to communicate by phone): please email reception@core-connections.ca with the subject line “Request to Book Through Email Only.” Include the type of appointment you are seeking and any communication accommodations you require. We will note your preferences in your profile and communicate with you by email. Reception email is monitored Monday to Friday, from 8:15 a.m. to 12:15 p.m. and again at approximately 4:00 p.m.

Visual impairments: please call reception at 613-291-2956 to let us know about your communication needs. We will note your preferences in your profile and provide appointment details and other relevant information verbally, as needed.

Our goal is to help you feel informed, supported and comfortable communicating with our team.
