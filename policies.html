Clinic policies

How we operate

We review these policies regularly. Reach out if anything here raises a question.

Your appointment time is reserved just for you. A late cancellation or missed appointment leaves a gap in your therapist's day that could have gone to another patient, so we ask for at least 48 business hours' notice to cancel or reschedule.

Cancellations must be made by phone at 613-291-2956, Monday to Friday, 8:30am-5:00pm. We're closed weekends and don't accept cancellations by email. In practice: a Monday appointment must be cancelled by noon Thursday, and a Tuesday appointment by noon Friday.

Fees

  • Less than 48 business hours' notice: 50% of the appointment fee
  • Missed appointment (no-show): 100% of the appointment fee

Exceptions

We make exceptions for sudden illness, family emergencies, and severe weather. We're not able to make exceptions for work conflicts. You can switch to a telehealth appointment any time by calling us, which avoids the fee entirely.

Late arrival

Call us if you're running late. We can't extend your appointment, but we'll treat you within the time remaining, and the full fee still applies.

All six of our physiotherapists are registered with the College of Physiotherapists of Ontario and comply with the Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA). We share your information only with your explicit written consent, except where the law requires disclosure.

Where your records live

Clinical records are stored electronically in Jane App, a PHIPA-compliant Canadian practice management system with servers in Montreal. Access is limited to our administrative staff, Jane App technical support, and the College of Physiotherapists of Ontario. Faxes are sent through SRFax, an encrypted, PIPEDA-compliant service. Paper files are shredded.

Email & phone

Your email and phone number are never sold or shared with third parties. We use them only for appointment reminders and clinic-related administrative communication.

Questions or concerns

Heather Grewar is our Health Information Custodian. Reach her at heather@core-connections.ca or 613-291-2956. For general privacy inquiries, contact the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Ontario at 416-326-3333.

Masks are available at our entrance and welcome any time, for any reason. If you're symptomatic, please wear a well-fitted mask or respirator to your appointment, or ask about switching to telehealth.

Please reschedule if you have

  • Fever, chills, or a new or worsening cough
  • Shortness of breath
  • Sore throat, congestion, or body aches unrelated to a known cause
  • Vomiting or diarrhea

If you're recovering from a recent respiratory illness, please attend only once your symptoms are improving, and wear a well-fitted mask.

What we do in clinic

  • Disinfect treatment surfaces between every client with Pre-Empt, a Health Canada-recommended hydrogen peroxide cleaner
  • Disinfect exercise equipment and modalities after each use, and high-touch areas daily
  • Follow best-practice hand hygiene before and after every patient contact
  • Run HEPA filters in every treatment room, and open windows when weather permits

Almost all of our services, including initial assessments, are available by telehealth. Our platform is secure, confidential, and PHIPA/PIPEDA-compliant, using the same 128-bit encryption standard most banks use.

Telehealth appointments cover the same ground as in-clinic ones: history-taking, visual assessment of posture and movement, education, exercise instruction, and follow-up. Fees match in-clinic pricing, and most Ontario extended health plans now cover virtual physiotherapy.

You can switch any appointment to telehealth by calling 613-291-2956, which also avoids a late cancellation fee. Possible risks include temporary audio or video disruptions or connectivity issues; your physiotherapist will confirm your location and check whether anyone else is home with you at the start of each session.

We're dedicated to an environment grounded in inclusion, diversity, and equity, where everyone is respected regardless of age, ancestry, ability, gender identity and expression, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

Our therapists and administrative staff have the right to a workplace of respectful conduct and communication. Inappropriate behaviour or aggressive language toward our team will not be tolerated, and may result in a refusal of care.

To report a concern, contact clinic owner Heather Grewar directly at heather@core-connections.ca.

We may ask your consent to use an AI scribe tool during your appointment, which drafts a clinical note from our conversation for your physiotherapist to review, edit, and sign. It lets your therapist focus on you instead of documentation.

The tool complies with Ontario's PHIPA. Only the clinician involved in your care can access the resulting notes, and the audio recording is permanently deleted afterward. Participation is entirely voluntary, and declining has no impact on your care.
